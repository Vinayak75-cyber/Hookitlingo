This page explains, in plain terms, the cookies and similar bits of storage Hookitlingo uses when you visit the site. For how we handle your personal information more broadly, see our Privacy Policy.

1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file a website stores in your browser. It lets the site remember something about you between visits, like whether you're logged in. Cookies aren't programs and can't run code on your device on their own.

2. Cookies we use

Cookie Purpose Type
hkl_user_session Keeps you logged in between visits. This one is required for the site to work; without it, you'd have to log in again on every page. Essential
_ga, _ga_* Set by Google Analytics to distinguish visitors and measure how people use the site (which pages get visited, how often, roughly where from). Analytics

We don't use cookies for advertising, and we don't sell any information gathered through cookies to third parties.

3. Local storage (not quite a cookie, but similar)

Some parts of Hookitlingo use your browser's local storage instead of a cookie. This includes:

  • Your light or dark theme preference
  • Lesson progress, XP, and badges as you work through a course

This information stays in your browser and isn't automatically sent to our servers. Clearing your browser's site data will reset it.

4. Managing cookies

Most browsers let you view, delete, or block cookies through their settings. Blocking the hkl_user_session cookie will prevent you from staying logged in. Blocking analytics cookies won't affect how the site functions for you, it just means we won't see your visit in our usage data.

If you'd rather opt out of Google Analytics specifically across the web, Google provides a browser add-on for that.

5. Changes to this policy

If the cookies we use change, such as adding a new analytics or feature tool, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

6. Contact us

Questions about our use of cookies? Reach out through our contact page.

Every lesson comes with quizzes, flashcards, and audio built right in. Progress, XP, and badges save locally in your browser as you work through each course.
