한국어Korean

Fun fact - Hangeul, Korea's alphabet, was designed in 1443 under King Sejong, one of the only writing systems on Earth with a known inventor and invention date.

Tip - Master Hangeul first. Once you can read it fluently, every course after it moves noticeably faster.

日本語Japanese

Fun fact - Japanese blends three scripts in a single sentence Hiragana, Katakana, and Kanji, each carrying a different job.

Tip - Hiragana and Katakana are each learnable in a weekend. Kanji is the long game that follows.
