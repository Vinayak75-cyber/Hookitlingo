Our Story

Learning a language is better together

We started Hookitlingo because we were tired of apps that teach you grammar rules and then leave you on your own. A language isn't really learned until you're using it with another person.

What we're building

Hookitlingo is a place to learn Korean and Japanese, and then actually use it. The lessons are structured with quizzes, flashcards, and audio, so you're not just reading, you're practicing from the first day. But the real point isn't the lessons. It's what comes after them: talking to a native speaker, meeting other learners, getting corrected by someone who actually knows the language instead of guessing at what an app tells you.

Why it matters

Maybe you're learning Korean because of a show you can't stop watching. Maybe it's Japanese for a trip you've been planning for years, or because of family, or a job, or honestly just because you like how it sounds. The reason doesn't really matter to us. What matters is that you get to speak it with someone before you've finished a whole textbook, not after.
